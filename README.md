# SHREE RANG KRUPA ENTERPRISE — INDUSTRIAL EQUIPMENT SOLUTIONS
### TAGLINE: POWERING EVERY LIFT.

A premium, modern, visually impressive, and fully responsive frontend business website for **SHREE RANG KRUPA ENTERPRISE**, an industrial equipment solutions company specializing in heavy-duty diesel forklift rental, loading/unloading operations, maintenance, repairs, and genuine spare parts.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Inspiration Upgrades

- **Clean Linear Navigation**: Single-line all-caps header (`HOME`, `SERVICES`, `EQUIPMENT`, `ABOUT US`, `CONTACT`) with active underline indicator, phone box with direct line (`+91 87808 29875`), and a prominent **`GET A QUOTE ↗`** CTA button with no line-wrapping.
- **Cinematic Machinery Showcase**: 
  - Central **Heavy-Duty Diesel Forklift** with ground contact drop-shadow and ambient reflection.
  - Interactive **Component Hotspot Pins** on the Mast, Engine Bay, Hydraulics, and Tyres with technical popovers.
  - Dynamic model counter, `Get in touch` phone pill, quick specs bar, and sleek circular `<` and `>` arrow navigation buttons.
- **Brand Palette — Light Industrial Edition**:
  - **Main Background**: `#F5F5F2`
  - **Section / Card Background**: `#FFFFFF`
  - **Primary Text**: `#20242A`
  - **Secondary Text**: `#667085`
  - **Primary Accent**: `#F5A623`
  - **Secondary Accent**: `#D98B12`
  - **Borders**: `#E5E7EB`

## 🏗️ Website Architecture & Sections

1. **Top Announcement Bar**: 24/7 hotline with direct call link, GIDC yard location, and instant WhatsApp link.
2. **Sticky Header**: Scalable SVG logo for Shree Rang Krupa Enterprise, single-line uppercase menu, direct phone button, and quote button.
3. **Hero Section**:
   - Centerpiece Diesel Forklift on industrial stage with ground shadow and reflection.
   - Interactive component hotspots (Triplex Mast, Turbo Diesel Engine, Tilt Cylinders, Puncture-Proof Tyres).
   - Circular `<` and `>` arrow navigation cycling 4 Fleet Models (3.5T, 5.0T, 7.0T, 16.0T).
   - Minimal `Get in touch` pill and quick specifications bar.
   - Heavy industrial metrics row (`3.0T – 16.0T`, `24/7`, `100% Inspected`, `10+ Sectors`).
4. **About Shree Rang Krupa Enterprise**: Asymmetric layout with industrial photography, brand story, and the 4 pillars.
5. **Our Services (5 Premier Cards)**: Diesel Forklift Rental, Loading & Unloading, Repairing, Maintenance, Spare Parts.
6. **Forklift Rental & Equipment**: Interactive model cards for 3.5T, 5.0T, 7.0T, and 16.0T models with comprehensive technical specifications.
7. **Why Choose Shree Rang Krupa Enterprise**: 6-card industrial strength grid with numbered tags (`01.` through `06.`).
8. **Industries We Serve**: 10 core industrial sectors with icons and descriptions.
9. **Contact & Enquiry Section**:
   - High-impact Industrial CTA banner.
   - Direct phone (`+91 87808 29875`), WhatsApp, location, and operational hours.

10. **Footer**: Logo, descriptor, services navigation, contact directory, and copyright.
11. **Mobile Quick-Action Bar**: Floating bottom pill with "Call Now", "WhatsApp", and "Quote" buttons.
